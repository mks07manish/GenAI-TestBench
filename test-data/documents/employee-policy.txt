Employee Leave and Work Policy

Annual Leave: Full-time employees receive 20 annual leave days per calendar year. Annual leave should be requested through the employee portal and approved by the employee's manager before leave begins.

Sick Leave: Employees receive 10 sick leave days per calendar year. If an employee is absent for more than three consecutive working days, Human Resources may request a medical certificate.

Remote Work: Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Some roles may require on-site attendance based on business needs.

Working Hours: The standard working day is eight hours, excluding breaks.

Expenses: Business travel expenses must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 days of the trip. Receipts are required for individual expenses above 1,000 currency units.

Information Security: Employees must not share passwords. Sensitive customer information must only be accessed for authorized business purposes. Suspicious emails should be reported to the IT security team.
